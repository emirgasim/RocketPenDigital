Frequently Asked Questions

What should be done first?

Define the audience and desired outcome for website design: step-by-step implementation guide. Review current evidence and identify the most important assumption to test.

How should priorities be chosen?

Compare potential impact, evidence confidence, implementation effort and maintenance cost. Begin with a manageable improvement linked to a real customer need.

How long do results take?

Timing depends on the channel, baseline and customer journey. Operational changes may show early signals, while search visibility, trust and retention usually need longer observation.

Which metrics matter?

Choose a few measures tied to the goal, define them consistently and interpret them with customer feedback. Avoid vanity metrics that do not support decisions.

Can small teams use this framework?

Yes. Start with a focused scope and clear ownership. Add specialist support or tools only when a demonstrated need justifies the complexity.
