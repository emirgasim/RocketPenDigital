Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main purpose of this approach?

It provides a structured way to make better decisions about brand architecture: organise products, services and sub-brands. The right implementation depends on audience needs, starting conditions, available resources and the quality of evidence used to guide decisions.

What should a small business do first?

Start with one clear objective and one audience need. Review what is already working, identify the most consequential gap and choose a manageable improvement that can be evaluated.

How long does it take to see results?

There is no reliable universal timeline. Some operational improvements can be assessed quickly, while search visibility, customer trust and complex buying journeys often take longer. Set milestones that match the feedback cycle.

Which metrics are useful?

Select measures linked to the objective, such as qualified visits, completed enquiries, engagement quality, adoption, retention or revenue contribution. Define them consistently and interpret them alongside customer feedback.

Can a team manage this internally?

Yes, when the team has clear ownership, sufficient capacity and the necessary skills. Document the workflow and seek specialist support where technical, creative, analytical or compliance gaps create material risk.
