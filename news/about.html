ABOUT THE NEWSROOM

About RocketPen Tech

RocketPen Tech is a technology-news publication being developed as part of the RocketPen brand. Its editions are designed for global English-language readers, audiences in Türkiye, and Arabic-speaking readers with particular relevance to the Gulf region.

What we cover

Artificial intelligence, consumer devices, apps and software, gaming, science, cybersecurity, startups, mobility, platforms and digital culture.

How we work

We aim to distinguish confirmed facts, attributed statements, analysis and uncertainty. Stories should include publication dates and accessible source links. Corrections should be made transparently.
