# Public-Web Keyword Research — RocketPen Digital
Date: 2026-10-22
Scope: English-language digital marketing / web services. This is a public-source research brief to supplement the first-pass URL audit. It is not Search Console data and does not prove this new domain is indexed.

## What public research suggests

1. A September 2026 article from AMS Digital reports US Google monthly averages sourced from DataForSEO / Google Ads data. It reports **“web design agency” at 16,667 monthly searches in June–August 2026**, compared with 7,667 in its 2023 baseline. This is one third-party source, not an independently reproduced export; treat it as directional evidence, not a guaranteed current volume for RocketPen Digital. Source: https://amsdigital.design/website-demand-2026
2. A web-services keyword dataset published by RGB, last measured 26 September 2026, reports global English-language estimates (no country filter) including “SEO services” at 135,000 and “SEO agency” at 74,000 monthly searches. The source says its volumes come from Google Ads and are global, so they must not be misread as US or UK volumes. Source: https://rgb.ir/en/trends/web-services/
3. Google Keyword Planner is Google's primary self-service source for keyword ideas, average monthly searches, and advertising competition; it requires account setup and location/language configuration. Official guide: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243
4. Google Trends measures relative search interest rather than exact absolute monthly counts and should be used to compare geography and trends, not to invent search-volume figures. Official guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/trends-start
5. For a new site, Google says discovery and indexing can take days to weeks; submitting a sitemap does not guarantee indexing or ranking. Official references: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7474347 and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl

## How to turn this into a real ranking plan

1. Review live first-page results for each candidate in the United States and United Kingdom separately; record page type, competitors, SERP features and what searchers appear to want.
2. Use Google Keyword Planner with location set to US, UK, Canada and Australia separately; export monthly search ranges, competition and top-of-page bid. Avoid combining countries into one misleading volume.
3. Map one primary query and a small set of close variants to each relevant URL. If a URL does not match a genuine service or lacks unique user value, do not force it into a commercial target.
4. Review the 216 template-family candidates for actual content duplication; a shared suffix is a flag, not a reason by itself to merge or delete.
5. Before any content consolidation, check status code, canonical, index status, inbound links and any existing traffic. For a new site these signals may be sparse, but they still must be checked where available.
6. Submit/confirm the sitemap in Search Console and request indexing for the homepage and a small representative set of priority pages. Do not mass-submit every URL repeatedly or promise indexing.
7. After data accumulates, update this hypothesis list using impressions, clicks, queries, qualified enquiries and conversions.

## Important limitations

- Search Console performance cannot be inferred from public web pages.
- The domain's age is based on the owner's statement that it launched yesterday; this report has not independently verified registration/deployment time.
- No reliable source found here provides defensible search volumes for all 500 exact target queries across all four countries.
- These recommendations do not guarantee indexing, rankings, traffic, leads or AI citations.
