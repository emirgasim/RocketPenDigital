# SEO Keyword Mapping & Cannibalization Review — Stage 2

Scope: 500 unique English article URLs found in the current sitemap. Generated 2026-10-22 from URL slugs only.

## Triage counts
- P1 commercial-intent candidates: 36
- P2 informational/query validation: 248
- P3 repeated-template similarity review: 216

## What this delivers
The CSV adds a candidate primary query, assumed intent, provisional topic/template cluster and required validation to every URL. It gives the next review a consistent order and makes suspected overlap visible.

## Limitations
Candidate queries and priority are heuristic labels inferred from slugs, not verified keyword research. No search volume, keyword difficulty, rankings, impressions, clicks, backlinks, conversions or AI citations are available in the repository. Do not treat this as proof that a phrase is popular or that pages in one template family should automatically be merged.
